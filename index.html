AI models for the pharmaceutical industry

AI models fully trained for the work of a pharmaceutical company.

Pharmaccelerator trains domain models for medical affairs, regulatory, market access, supply chain, commercial and strategy teams. Each model is implemented on-premises or in a private cloud, where the company's information stays fully under its control, with governance and industry policies built in.

Medical affairs

Scientific response drafting, field insight synthesis, publication and congress monitoring.

Regulatory

Submission authoring support across CTD modules, agency query responses, labelling consistency checks.

Market access

HTA appraisal analysis, value dossiers, pricing and reimbursement scenarios by market.

Supply chain

Demand and shortage signals, batch record review, supplier and quality documentation.

Commercial

Account and territory intelligence, compliant content review, launch readiness.

Strategy

Portfolio and asset assessment, competitive intelligence, scenario planning.

Under your control, by design

Every model is delivered as a product the company owns, implemented where its information already lives, and operated under the governance and industry policies the organisation is held to.

Deployment

On-premises or private cloud

Implemented in your own data centre or in a private cloud of your choice. Prompts, documents, outputs and weights stay inside your environment.

Control

Information fully under your control

Governance

Governance and industry policies

Role-based access, audit trails and model documentation, with the policies your industry works under applied: GxP and computer system validation, pharmacovigilance and promotional-code rules, GDPR and the EU AI Act.

Ownership

A product you own

The trained model is yours, not a subscription to us. A specialised partner packages it into an optimised infrastructure for the hardware and setup agreed with you.
