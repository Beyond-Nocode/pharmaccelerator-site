AI models for the economic value of medicines

We train AI models that understand what a medicine is worth.

Pharmaccelerator trains domain models on health economics and outcomes research: HTA appraisals, economic evaluations, pricing and reimbursement decisions, and real-world outcomes. So that payers, manufacturers, investors and health systems can each assess the value of a medicine from their own perspective, with the evidence in view.

One medicine, seven value questions

Every stakeholder reads the same clinical and cost evidence. The perspective sets which costs and benefits are allowed to count, and that is what turns one evidence base into seven different values.

  1. One medicineShared evidenceclinical effect and safety · costs · outcomes and utilities
  2. ResearchUniversities and research centresIs this research worth funding, and what value could it unlock? Counts value potential and evidence gaps.
  3. PayerPayers and HTA bodiesIs it worth reimbursing at this price? Counts payer costs and QALYs gained.
  4. CommercialManufacturersWhat price does the evidence support? Counts price, volume and time to access.
  5. FinancialInvestorsWhat is the asset worth? Counts risk-adjusted cash flows.
  6. SocietalSociety and policy makersIs the welfare gain worth the cost to society? Counts productivity, carers and equity.
  7. PatientPatients and caregiversDoes it improve what matters to me, at a cost I can bear? Counts quality of life and out-of-pocket cost.
  8. ProviderHealth systems and providersWhat does adoption cost, and what does it displace? Counts budget, staff time and beds.
